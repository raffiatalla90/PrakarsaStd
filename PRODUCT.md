# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Laravel 13 + React 19 via Inertia, Vite, plain CSS in `resources/css/app.css`. Chosen by the user.

## Users

Business owners and teams from any industry (no single niche) who need a website: company profile, landing page, or custom web app. Confirmed by the user: "klien segala lini".

## Product Purpose

Prakarsa Studio is a startup web development studio that fuses web development with AI. The site is its company profile: it must explain what the studio does and move a visitor to start a conversation through the contact form.

## Positioning

Slogan: "Prakarsa Studio: Initiating Tomorrow's Website Solution". Tagline: "The First Step in Digital Innovation." Mechanism per the brand deck: web development fused with AI so brands move first in their industry.

## Operating Context

Reference site named by the user: nirmatech.com (same service category). Content language is Indonesian, slogans stay English. Contact form posts to Laravel `/kontak` and stores to `contact_messages`; optional email via `CONTACT_RECEIVER`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services in the site today (drafted from the brand deck, user has not yet confirmed wording): website building, UI/UX design, AI automation and integration. Four-step working process (Pahami, Rancang, Bangun, Luncurkan) is also a draft.

## Brand Commitments

Binding, from the user's media branding deck and moodboard:
- Palette 1: Palladian #EEE9DF, Oatmeal #C9C1B1, Burning Flame #FFB162, Truffle Trouble #A35139.
- Palette 2: Blue Fantastic #2C3B4D, Anchorfish Blue #1B2632, Burning Flame #FFB162, Truffle Trouble #A35139.
- Fonts: DM Sans for headlines, Averia Serif Libre for body.
- Logos: three supplied variants (full wordmark, "STD." mark, P mark). Site uses full logo and the P crop for favicon.
- Visual references from the deck: stepped square blocks (cover slide), dotted rule, orange glow gradients on dark (reference image), cream-to-orange and navy-to-orange gradient cards.
- Tone for awareness content in the deck: informative, relatable, engaging.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No portfolio, no client names, no testimonials, no team photos, no prices, no statistics, no public contact email or WhatsApp. Future work must not fabricate any of these.

## Product Principles

1. Show the studio's craft through the site itself, since there is no portfolio to show.
2. Say only what is true: no invented clients, numbers, or guarantees.
3. One clear action: start a conversation.
4. Brand palette and fonts are fixed; form and motion are free.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Indonesian-language audience, mobile-heavy. Meet WCAG AA contrast and keyboard operability.
